About MindTrack

MindTrack is a machine learning project. A scikit-learn model, served through FastAPI, estimates a student's mental health score from age, country, academic level, social media habits, study time, physical activity, sleep and stress level.

The score is a model output based on patterns in survey data. It is not a diagnosis and should not be used to make decisions about a person's health.
